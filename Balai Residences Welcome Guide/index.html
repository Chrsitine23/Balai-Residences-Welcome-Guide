How to Use the Washing Machine

Instructions

  1. Plug in the Washing Machine
  2. Open the Faucet
  3. Place the clothes inside the drum
  4. Add the recommended amount of detergent
  5. Press the power button on the Washing Machine
  6. Turn the dial and select Quick 30
  7. Press the Start/Pause button
  8. Wait until the washing cycle is complete
  9. After the wash finishes, select Rinse + Spin and Press Start/Pause Again
  10. Once the cycle is finish, remove the clothes gently from the drum

Activities

Things to Do Around Mactan Newtown

  • Mactan Newtown Beach

    Enjoy a relaxing day by the beach, swim, take photos, or simply unwind by the sea. The beach is located within Mactan Newtown and is approximately 10–15 minutes on foot from the building.

  • Mactan Newtown Boardwalk

    Take a relaxing walk around the township and enjoy the seaside views, landscaped streets, restaurants, cafés, and the laid-back atmosphere of Mactan Newtown.

  • Dining & Food Trips

    Explore the restaurants, cafés, and food spots around Mactan Newtown. Enjoy Filipino, Korean, Western, and international dishes within walking distance.

  • Cafés & Chill Spots

    Grab coffee, enjoy dessert, work remotely, or relax at one of the many cafés around Mactan Newtown.

Nearby Mactan Attractions

  • Mactan Shrine

    Visit the historic Mactan Shrine and see the Lapu-Lapu Monument, Magellan Marker, and Battle of Mactan landmarks. A great place to learn more about Cebu's history.

  • Mactan World Museum

    Discover Filipino and Spanish heritage through exhibits, historical displays, and stories about Lapu-Lapu and the Battle of Mactan.

  • Island Hopping & Snorkeling

    Explore the waters around Mactan and nearby islands. Enjoy snorkeling, swimming, diving, and island-hopping tours available around Mactan.

  • Water Sports

    Try different water activities around Mactan Island, including snorkeling, diving, and other ocean adventures.

  • Cebu Yacht Club

    A premier marina in Mactan offering scenic waterfront views, yacht and boat activities, and a relaxing atmosphere. A great spot for dining, sunset views, and enjoying the seaside vibe.

  • 10K Roses

    A beautiful seaside attraction in Cordova known for its stunning field of LED roses, especially magical around sunset. Perfect for photos, relaxing, and enjoying the waterfront views.

Relax & Enjoy Mactan

  • Spa & Massage

    Relax after a day of exploring with a massage or spa treatment. Several spas are located within a short distance of Mactan Newtown.

  • Shopping & Essentials

    Explore the shops and retail areas around Mactan Newtown for snacks, essentials, souvenirs, and everyday needs.

  • Sunset Walk

    Take an evening walk around the Newtown waterfront and enjoy the sunset and relaxing seaside atmosphere.

Island Adventures

  • Olango Island

    Take a boat trip to Olango Island and explore its natural surroundings and wildlife sanctuary.

  • Maribago Beaches

    Explore the beaches and resort areas around Maribago, just a short drive from Mactan Newtown.

  • Diving & Marine Adventures

    Mactan is a popular starting point for diving, snorkeling, and marine adventures around Cebu's coastline.

Mactan Newtown offers plenty of options for relaxing, dining, exploring, and enjoying the beach. Plan your activities according to your available time and preferred experience.

Generator

Generator During Power Outage

During a power outage, the generator need to be on Manually to provide continuous power.

Please note that the generator only supports selected essential outlets and appliances.

What will continue to work:
  • Refrigerator designated outlet
  • One living room light
  • One convenience outlet
  • Wi-Fi connection

These limitations help ensure that essential services remain available throughout your stay.

During a Power Outage

To help avoid any inconvenience and protect the appliances from possible power surges or electrical instability, please follow the step below.

Turn OFF the MAIN BREAKER.

This helps protect the appliances from possible power surges or electrical instability when electricity is interrupted.

When Power Returns

  1. Wait 3–5 minutes.
    Allow the electricity to stabilize.
  2. Turn ON the MAIN BREAKER.

Important Reminder

The generator is designed to provide power only to the designated essential outlets listed above. Other appliances and outlets may remain unavailable during an outage.

Please make sure the MAIN BREAKER is turned OFF during an outage and only turn it back ON after waiting 3–5 minutes once power has returned.

Thank you for helping us protect the appliances and prevent inconvenience during power interruptions. 🤎
